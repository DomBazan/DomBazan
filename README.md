👋 Hi, I’m Dominick Bazan — a frontend developer who likes building things that look good, work smoothly, and actually help people.

I’m studying Transformational Leadership and Social Impact at ESADE, where I’m exploring how technology, leadership, and community can come together. Alongside my studies, I’m part of Tech Labs, where I help teach coding, and Okios, where I work in HR and support community-service initiatives.

I’m currently building Pet Pal, a website concept designed to help animal adoption centers connect pets with potential adopters. I’m also working on websites for a restaurant and a shop, while polishing my earlier projects and strengthening my GitHub portfolio.

My next goals are to build more interactive games, explore AI-powered projects, and collaborate with a team on something ambitious.

Outside of coding, I’m a third-degree dan in Taekwondo and also practice sword martial arts — so I bring discipline, focus, and a little bit of intensity to everything I build. ⚔️

* 🌍  I'm based in Utah, USA · Currently studying in Sant Cugat, Spain.
